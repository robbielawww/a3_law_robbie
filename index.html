02 / Beer Families

A little beer knowledge. A better pour.

Lagers, Ales, Wheat Beers, and Stout

Lagers

Crisp, clean and easy drinking, a refreshing place to start.

Ales

Expressive, from fruity and bright to rich and malty.

Wheat Beers

Soft, often hazy and refreshing, with bready or citrus notes.

Stout

Dark, rich-bodied beer with flavors of coffee and dark chocolate.

03 / Top 10

Bob's Top Beer

Personal picks

  • #1 Busch - Busch takes the top spot overall because it delivers the ideal combination of cost and great quality. It’s a smooth beer with real flavor without having to compromise on getting a great beer.
    Go Buy Busch
  • #2 Bud Light
  • #3 Miller - Miller is the best light beer around, offering crisp, refreshing flavor. Its slightly higher price tag keeps it just a couple of spots lower, though it remains an easy go to.
  • #4 Coors
  • #5 Corona - Corona is the smoothest, tastiest beer in my lineup for kicking back and relaxing. For the best experience, serve it ice cold with a fresh lime.
  • #6 Stella
  • #7 Modelo - Modelo brings a thicker, smoother taste that stands out on a hot day. It is very refreshing making it an easy favorite when hot outside.
  • #8 Budweiser
  • #9 Peroni - Peroni is a crisp beer that hits the spot when you are hungry. Clean and refreshing, it tastes amazing with barbecue food.
  • #10 Sol

04 / Styles

What's in your glass?

Pilsner, Amber lager, Pale Ale, IPA, Wheat Beer, and Stout.

  • Pilsner - Lager - Crisp malt with a clean, slightly hoppy finish.
  • Amber Lager - Lager - Toasted malt with balanced hops and a smooth finish.
  • Pale Ale - Ale - Balanced malt with hop character.
  • IPA - Ale - Tastes of hops and bitterness.
  • Wheat Beer - Wheat - Soft texture with light grain or citrus notes.
  • Stout - Dark - Rich character with roasted coffee and chocolate notes.
